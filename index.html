    What do these mean?

    Tasbīḥ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِSubḥānAllāh“Glory be to Allah” — declaring Allah free of any fault.
    Taḥmīd الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِAlḥamdulillāh“All praise is for Allah” — thanking Him for everything.
    Takbīr اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُAllāhu Akbar“Allah is the Greatest” — greater than all else.
    Tahlīl لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُLā ilāha illā-llāh“There is no god but Allah” — the testimony of faith.
    Istighfār أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَAstaghfirullāh“I seek Allah's forgiveness.”

    How to pray

    A simple walkthrough of one prayer (2 rakʿahs, like Fajr). Longer prayers repeat the same unit. This is a general guide; small details differ between schools.

    Rakʿahs in each prayer
    Fajr 2Dhuhr 4ʿAsr 4Maghrib 3ʿIshāʾ 4
    1Intention & standing
    Stand facing the Qibla, feet apart, and intend in your heart which prayer you are about to perform. Intention is in the heart — it isn't said aloud.
    2Takbīr — begin
    Raise your hands to your ears/shoulders and say:
    اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
    Allāhu Akbar
    “Allah is the Greatest.” Then place your right hand over your left on your chest.
    3Recite Al-Fātiḥah
    بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ۝ اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ ۝ صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ
    Bismillāhi-r-Raḥmāni-r-Raḥīm. Al-ḥamdu lillāhi Rabbi-l-ʿālamīn. Ar-Raḥmāni-r-Raḥīm. Māliki yawmi-d-dīn. Iyyāka naʿbudu wa iyyāka nastaʿīn. Ihdina-ṣ-ṣirāṭa-l-mustaqīm. Ṣirāṭa-lladhīna anʿamta ʿalayhim, ghayri-l-maghḍūbi ʿalayhim wa la-ḍ-ḍāllīn.
    “In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate. All praise is for Allah, Lord of the worlds; the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate; Master of the Day of Judgement. You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. Guide us to the straight path — the path of those You have blessed, not those who earned anger, nor those who went astray.” Then say Āmīn.
    In the first two rakʿahs, follow it with a short sūrah, e.g. Al-Ikhlāṣ (Qul huwa-llāhu aḥad…).
    4Rukūʿ — bowing
    Say “Allāhu Akbar”, bow with your back straight and hands on your knees, and say three times:
    سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ
    Subḥāna Rabbiya-l-ʿAẓīm ×3
    “Glory be to my Lord, the Most Great.”
    5Stand up straight
    Rise from bowing, saying:
    سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ، رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ
    Samiʿa-llāhu liman ḥamidah, Rabbanā wa laka-l-ḥamd
    “Allah hears whoever praises Him. Our Lord, to You is all praise.”
    6Sujūd — prostration
    Say “Allāhu Akbar” and prostrate with forehead, nose, palms, knees and toes on the ground. Say three times:
    سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
    Subḥāna Rabbiya-l-Aʿlā ×3
    “Glory be to my Lord, the Most High.” Sit up (“Rabbi-ghfir lī” — my Lord, forgive me), then prostrate a second time the same way.
    7Second rakʿah
    Stand and do it all again: Al-Fātiḥah, a short sūrah, rukūʿ, standing, and two sujūd. After the second sujūd of the 2nd rakʿah, sit for the Tashahhud.
    8Tashahhud — sitting
    التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَى عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ
    At-taḥiyyātu lillāhi wa-ṣ-ṣalawātu wa-ṭ-ṭayyibāt. As-salāmu ʿalayka ayyuha-n-nabiyyu wa raḥmatu-llāhi wa barakātuh. As-salāmu ʿalaynā wa ʿalā ʿibādi-llāhi-ṣ-ṣāliḥīn. Ashhadu an lā ilāha illa-llāh, wa ashhadu anna Muḥammadan ʿabduhu wa rasūluh.
    “All greetings, prayers and good things are for Allah. Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and Allah's mercy and blessings. Peace be upon us and upon the righteous servants of Allah. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and that Muhammad is His servant and messenger.” Point the right index finger at the testimony.
    9Ṣalawāt on the Prophet
    اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ
    Allāhumma ṣalli ʿalā Muḥammad wa ʿalā āli Muḥammad, kamā ṣallayta ʿalā Ibrāhīm wa ʿalā āli Ibrāhīm, innaka Ḥamīdun Majīd…
    “O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and his family as You blessed Ibrāhīm and his family; You are Praiseworthy, Glorious.” (Said in the final sitting.)
    10Taslīm — ending
    Turn your face to the right, then to the left, saying each time:
    السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ
    As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatu-llāh
    “Peace and Allah's mercy be upon you.” The prayer is complete.

    For a 3-rakʿah (Maghrib) or 4-rakʿah prayer, sit briefly for the first Tashahhud after 2 rakʿahs, stand to finish the remaining rakʿahs (reciting only Al-Fātiḥah), then do the final Tashahhud, Ṣalawāt and Taslīm.
